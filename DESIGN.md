# Dim Sum Atlas design direction

This is the current source of truth for the Pages product surface. It uses Material 3 anatomy with a calm tea-house catalogue feel: warm jade as the accent, porcelain neutrals for surfaces, expressive rounded containers, and compact editorial metadata.

## Defaults

- Product surface: browsing and inspecting a large dim-sum image catalogue.
- Personality: calm tea-house and editorial catalogue.
- Motion: subtle and non-blocking.
- User control: theme, density, font scale, corner radius, spacing, accent, language, funny levels, presets, import, export, and reset are available in Settings.

This direction is intentionally user-customizable and should evolve through the live appearance editor rather than through hard-coded one-off overrides.
